# Rover homepage

The homepage uses a 16-second web animation (not an encoded video): drive-in at 0–5 seconds, exploded component reveal at 5–9 seconds, and shop exploration at 9–16 seconds. Pause, replay, and individual stage controls are provided. Background tabs pause the timeline. Reduced-motion users see a static component reveal.

The illustrated gallery covers 14 component families. It does not create inventory or claim that additional items are in stock. Families without existing catalog records lead to the contact page. Existing catalog products retain their prices and links.

Seven additional images were generated with the built-in image-generation tool: rover, motors, power, displays, LEDs, switches/connectors, and semiconductors. Output assets are under public/images/components. The generation brief requested realistic studio 3D product illustrations, a pale blue-white background, generous margins, and no text or watermarks. These are illustrative rather than exact engineering references.

Live Supabase connection and real inventory remain pending from the initial build.
